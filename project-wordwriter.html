Overview

WordWriter is an AI-powered writing assistant built for high-quality book writing and long-form content creation. My work focused on making a genuinely complex tool — one juggling AI generation, editing, and structure — feel calm, focused, and writer-friendly.

This is placeholder content — send over the real problem statement, process shots, and outcomes from your case study, and I'll drop them straight in here.

The Challenge

Most AI writing tools optimize for short-form output and end up cluttered with prompts, panels, and settings. Writers working on long-form projects — books, chapters, ongoing manuscripts — needed an interface that stayed out of their way while still making AI assistance feel present and useful exactly when needed.

My Process

I focused on the core writing surface first: a distraction-free editor with AI assistance surfaced contextually rather than through heavy toolbars. From there, I designed the surrounding structure — chapter organization, version history, and export flows — to support the realities of writing something long.

Outcome

The result is a writing environment that balances AI capability with the quiet focus long-form writing actually requires — an ongoing project I continue to design for today.
