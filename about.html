About Me

Samuel Obiora

Hi, I'm Samuel and I'm a Product Designer. I'm in love with making digital tools easier and more pleasant to use through functional and wonderful design.

My Story

I believe that design helps us experience life clearly. It is the most human, scientific tool to understand humanity.

When I was 6, my mother took my siblings and me to see her mother, our grandma. The visit was mostly boring, and I spent most of my afternoons and evenings sitting by the corridor watching cars fly by down the road — Mercedes-Benz, Volvo, Toyota, different shapes and sizes. I soon could tell each car apart without seeing their logos.

I didn't know it then, but my little brain had hooked into the essence of design and branding.

How I Work

Design with Strategy and Creativity

My process blends strategy and creativity to address challenges, craft solutions, and deliver designs that effectively communicate your message.

01

Research & Strategy

I dive deep into understanding your business, target audience, and project goals. Through research and strategic planning, I create a clear roadmap to guide the entire design process.

02

Concept & Ideation

I brainstorm and develop creative concepts that align with your vision. Initial sketches and ideas are refined into tangible wireframes, setting the direction for design and functionality.

03

Feedback & Refinement

Collaboration is key. I review the design with you, gather feedback, and refine the work to align with your expectations and goals.

04

Testing & Optimization

I conduct thorough testing to identify and resolve any performance or usability issues, ensuring the design works seamlessly across devices.

05

Launch & Delivery

Once everything is finalized, the project is launched and delivered to you, with guidance and support for ongoing maintenance.
