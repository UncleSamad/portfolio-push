ClientSalesGroup
RoleProduct & UX Designer
Timeline—
FocusUX Strategy, UI Design, Design System
SalesGroup

Overview

SalesGroup is an always-on AI assistant that converts website visitors, answers customer questions, and collects real reviews — so businesses grow revenue while customers feel taken care of. My role was to shape the end-to-end product experience: from the client-facing chat widget to the internal dashboard business owners use to manage conversations and insights.

The Challenge

Small and mid-sized businesses were losing revenue to unanswered questions and abandoned carts, but couldn't justify a full-time support team. SalesGroup needed an interface that felt less like "a chatbot" and more like a reliable employee — trustworthy, fast, and easy for non-technical business owners to configure.

My Process

I led research and design across the core flows: onboarding, chat widget configuration, review collection, and the analytics dashboard. Working closely with engineering, I turned early wireframes into a cohesive design system that could scale across new features without losing consistency.

"Samuel took my ideas and made them into a working product. More than colours and text, he set up our design infrastructure and design system."

Outcome

The redesigned platform shipped with a clearer onboarding flow, a more trustworthy chat experience, and a dashboard that made it easy for business owners to see impact at a glance — contributing to stronger customer retention and satisfaction.
