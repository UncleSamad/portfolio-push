Turning an AI Sports Prediction Engine Into a Human-Focused Tool

ClientMagicJar
RoleProduct Designer
Timeline—
FocusUX Design, Data Visualization
MagicJar

Overview

MagicJar is powered by a sophisticated AI sports prediction engine — but raw model output isn't a product. My job was to translate dense statistical predictions into something sports fans could actually understand, trust, and enjoy using.

This is placeholder content — send over the real problem statement, process shots, and outcomes from your case study, and I'll drop them straight in here.

The Challenge

The underlying engine produced accurate but highly technical predictions. The challenge was designing an experience that felt exciting and easy to scan — not like a spreadsheet of probabilities — while still being honest about confidence levels and uncertainty.

My Process

I designed a visual language for presenting predictions — using clear hierarchy, confidence indicators, and match-day context — so users could get a read on a game in seconds. I worked closely with the data team to make sure the simplified UI never misrepresented what the model was actually saying.

Outcome

The result turned a powerful but inaccessible prediction engine into a product people could actually engage with day-to-day — bridging the gap between AI output and human understanding.
